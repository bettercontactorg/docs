---
title: 'Enrich leads'
description: Submit up to 100 leads for waterfall email and phone enrichment.
openapi: 'POST /async'
---

<Note>
  Provide `first_name`, `last_name` and either `company` or `company_domain`. `company_domain` gives
  materially better results than a company name. Add `linkedin_url` whenever you have it, and always
  when you request `enrich_phone_number`.
</Note>

<Warning>
  This endpoint is asynchronous. A `201` means the batch was accepted, not that it was enriched.
  Keep the returned `id` and read [Request statuses](/api-reference/statuses) before you build your
  polling loop.

  Submissions are **not idempotent**: retrying a `POST` that had actually succeeded enriches the
  same leads twice and consumes credits twice.
</Warning>

Anything you put in `custom_fields` comes back untouched in the results, which is the simplest way
to reconcile enriched leads with your own records.
